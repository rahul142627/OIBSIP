# Task 1 — EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Track
Data Analytics (Level 1)

## Objective
Perform a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to uncover
sales patterns, customer behavior, product performance, and key business insights.

## Dataset
Retail sales transaction dataset containing customer demographics (age, gender), location
(country, state), product details (category, sub-category, product), order quantity, unit
cost/price, and financials (revenue, cost, profit) across multiple transaction dates.

## Tech Stack
Python, pandas, numpy, matplotlib, seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

## Key Steps Performed
- Data loading and initial inspection
- Descriptive statistics on numerical columns
- Time series analysis of sales trends across months and quarters
- Customer demographic analysis (age group and gender breakdown)
- Product and category performance analysis
- Correlation analysis between revenue, cost, and profit
- Revenue breakdown by age group and gender
- Business recommendations based on findings

## Key Insights
- Adult customers are the largest and most valuable customer segment
- A small number of products generate a significant share of total revenue
- Sales show seasonal fluctuations across different months and quarters
- Revenue is strongly associated with Profit and Cost
- Customer purchases are fairly balanced across genders
